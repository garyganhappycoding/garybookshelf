# 陪你读书 · Form 5 · Bahasa Melayu (BM) · weekly vocabulary (15 words) and essays

Dates are for a Group B state such as KL; Group A states start one day earlier. Exam dates are ESTIMATES (each student can change them and the weeks move). Exam weeks carry no vocabulary and no tasks; the whole week moves to the first week after the exams.

**Form 5 vocabulary:** 15 words a week. About half advanced words (7 or 8, Table 2: peribahasa and fine phrases) and half ordinary words (Table 1). Where a topic has too few advanced words, ordinary words fill in and the week says GAP.

**GAP: the Form 5 SPM-sprint plan, the Form 5 essays (copied from Form 4 for now) and the Form 5 end date are still to be designed.**

Weeks of content: **36**.
