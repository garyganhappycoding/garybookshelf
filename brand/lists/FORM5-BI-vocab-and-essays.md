# 陪你读书 · Form 5 · English (BI) · weekly vocabulary (15 words) and essays

Dates are for a Group B state such as KL; Group A states start one day earlier. Exam dates are ESTIMATES (each student can change them and the weeks move). Exam weeks carry no vocabulary and no tasks; the whole week moves to the first week after the exams.

**Form 5 vocabulary:** 15 words a week. Level ratio about B2 : B2 to C1 : C1 = 2 : 3 : 5 (3 B2 words, 4 or 5 'B2 to C1' words, 7 or 8 C1 words). The packs only tag words B2 or C1, so I defined the middle tier as C1-tagged phrases of 1 to 2 words and the top tier as C1-tagged phrases of 3 or more words. Where a theme has too few words in a tier, another tier fills in and the week says GAP.

**GAP: the Form 5 SPM-sprint plan, the Form 5 essays (copied from Form 4 for now) and the Form 5 end date are still to be designed.**

Weeks of content: **36**.
