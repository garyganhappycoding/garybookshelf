# 陪你读书 · English (BI) vocab by week (version 2)

How the 10 words are chosen (Gary's rule):

- **Round 1 (B2)**: universal phrases first, the ones students can use in almost any question of the theme. These are the pack's "Universal phrases" (section 5). Where a pack has fewer clean phrases, the rest are common B2 keywords from the pack's ready answers.
- **Round 2 (B2 to C1)**: 5 more B2 phrases, then 5 higher-level phrases and nouns.
- **Round 3 and 4 (C1)**: higher-level phrases and nouns from the elaboration part of the pack (the answer keywords and the trial-paper vocab), picked because they are reusable and sound good in a model answer.

Marks: **‡** after a Chinese meaning = the pack gives only the English phrase, so I added the Chinese. Please check those. All other Chinese meanings and the B2/C1 levels are copied from the pack (the pack itself says they were written by Claude, so please check them too). Some universal phrases in the watermarked PDFs were scrambled; I rebuilt them from the example sentences.
