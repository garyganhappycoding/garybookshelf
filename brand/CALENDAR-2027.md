## Month by month (Claude's earlier draft)
Peak = lots of students watching, push notes. Prep = make and test the product before the peak. Warm = off-peak, build trust and the community.

| Month | Season | Batch 09 (Form 5) | Batch 10 (Form 4) | Push / do |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Rule of thumb: **peak months = sales content** (notes, packs, countdowns). **Warm months = trust content** (the artisan series, Telegram letters, behind the scenes). **Prep months = build** the products so peak months only need promotion.
