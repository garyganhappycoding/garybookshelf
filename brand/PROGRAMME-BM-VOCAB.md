How the weeks run: weeks 1 to 19 = one week per topic, 10 ordinary words each. Weeks 20 to 37 = each topic again, 5 ordinary + 5 advanced (peribahasa and fine phrases). Weeks 38 to 44 = the 7 topics with the most material, advanced words. Short, reusable phrases come first. A topic that has fewer advanced words than needed is topped up with ordinary words and marked.
