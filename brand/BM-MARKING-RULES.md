2. Superprof blog "SPM BM marking scheme" (a private tutoring site, NOT official): only its band names, mark ranges and deduction rules. Everything taken from it is marked **[unverified]**.

## Paper facts (KMJ 2025, official)
- Kertas 1 = 100 marks, 2 hours. **Bahagian A**: 1 compulsory question, 30 marks, based on a stimulus (graphic), 150 to 200 words. **Bahagian B**: 4 questions, answer 1, 70 marks, 350 to 500 words.
- The questions tell the student to **huraikan** (explain) reasons, effects, roles or ways. A question may ask for the points "selain" (other than) one given in the passage: the student must NOT write the given point.

## What the examiners call a strong answer (Prestasi Tinggi), paraphrased
- **Menepati tugasan**: answers exactly what the task asks (e.g. only the factors that are not already in the passage).
- **Idea**: clear AND mature, each idea explained with a suitable example. Every part of the stimulus is touched (as a main idea, a side idea, an example, the opening or the closing).
- **Bahasa**: grammatical words and sentences (kata dan ayat), wide and precise vocabulary (kosa kata), correct punctuation (tanda baca), varied language features (unsur bahasa bervariasi), register (laras) that fits the task, very few mistakes.
- **Gaya bahasa**: beautiful and effective expressions (ungkapan menarik), **peribahasa that fit exactly** (examples the examiners praise: "duri dalam daging", "melentur buluh biarlah dari rebungnya", "nila setitik rosak susu sebelanga", "duduk sama rendah, berdiri sama tinggi"), good sentence variety.
- **Pengolahan**: very interesting and effective.
- **Pemerengganan**: balanced and complete: perenggan pengenalan, perenggan idea, perenggan penutup.
- **Pengenalan**: shows that the student understood the task.
- **Penutup**: a firm conclusion that sums up the points and adds a suggestion and a hope (cadangan dan harapan).

## What a middle answer (Prestasi Sederhana) looks like, paraphrased
Still answers the task; ideas relevant and explained clearly with fitting examples; sentences still grammatical but with some small mistakes (examples they quote: a slightly wrong word choice such as "mengiktirafkan" for "mewartakan", "mempengaruhi" and the colloquial "mengecek" instead of "menyemak", "samada" instead of "sama ada", "memilikan" instead of "memiliki", "kandungan ibu mengandung"); vocabulary general and limited; language features show only a little variety; paragraphs still suitable; the closing still gives a suggestion and a hope.

## Weak answers
KMJ says some candidates fail to master writing and cannot express ideas well and effectively: off-task ideas, unclear explanation, many errors.

Other rules claimed by that site and NOT applied automatically: spelling 0.5 mark per error (capped at 3), format up to 2 marks, and caps for very short answers. The AI only reports the word count and lowers the mark for a short answer; it does not apply these numbers. Official mark ranges are not public in the KMJ.

## How the AI works
- It gives an **estimated** band and mark for practice, never an official mark. It says so.
- It judges the whole pattern, not single slips. Every mark is explained with the student's real words (no invented quotes).
- Five areas: Isi dan contoh, Bahasa, Gaya bahasa dan peribahasa, Pengolahan dan pemerengganan, Penutup.
- It lists the mistakes that cost marks with a fix (ejaan, imbuhan, kata hubung, ayat tidak gramatis, kata tidak baku), at most 8, and the good phrases to keep.
- Feedback is in simple Chinese with the Malay words quoted, like the rest of the programme.
- Only admin and beta testers can use it for now (Gary: BM to be reviewed first).
