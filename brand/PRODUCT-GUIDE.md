## What the AI essay marking gives (free vs paid)
- Free (10 essays per person, once): estimated mark out of 20, four criteria, the reason for every mark, a check on every point of the question.
- Paid (any pack, or the writing冲刺包 credits): also how to reach the next mark, the essay with earned and lost words highlighted, and the biggest gain. While you hold credits, every essay gets the full report.
- Always an estimate, not an official mark.
