# Telegram channel plan (draft, 17 Nov 2026)

Purpose: a place Gary owns, to reach students directly without depending on Instagram's reach. One-way broadcast, personal tone (friendly senior), mostly Chinese with English and Malay mixed.

## Part 1. How to open it (about 10 minutes, on your phone)
1. Telegram app, tap the pencil / menu icon, choose **New Channel**.
2. **Name:** something students recognise, e.g. "garybookshelf | Gary 的读书笔记" (you can change it later).
3. **Description (max 255 characters):** "Gary 的私人频道。新视频、新笔记、考试倒数和我自己的读书故事，不靠 Instagram 算法，直接告诉你。"
4. **Photo:** the blue shark logo (`public/dolphin-icon.png`).
5. Choose **Public** and pick a link such as `t.me/gary_bookshelf` (if taken, try `t.me/garybookshelf07`). Public lets you put one link in your Instagram bio and every reel.
6. Skip "add members" for now. You cannot add your 6k group members in bulk; they join by tapping your link.
7. Open the channel, tap its name, **Edit**:
   - **Discussion:** link your existing Telegram group so students can comment under each post (optional; recommended, it shows the community).
   - **Sign Messages:** leave off, so posts come from "garybookshelf".
   - **Reactions:** turn on, so students can tap an emoji (a free feedback signal).
8. Write and **pin** the welcome post (Part 3) before you share the link.
9. To post ahead of time: type the message, press and hold the send button, choose **Schedule Message**. Batch a week of posts on your Tuesday work day.

## Part 2. How students find it
- Post the link once in the existing 6k Telegram group and pin it there.
- Instagram bio link, and a one-line call to action at the end of reels: "新视频和笔记我会第一时间在 Telegram 频道告诉你".
- A card on the website (the Start here page and the footer) with the channel button.
- Keep collecting emails on the website too, as a back-up. Telegram is the home, email is insurance.

## Part 4. Weekly rhythm (about 20 minutes a week)
| Post | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Letter from Gary | 1 a week | 5 to 8 lines, personal: what I noticed this week, one tip, one question to the students |
| New reel / video link | Whenever published | One line on why it matters, then the link |
| Product or notes news | Only in peak months or when new | What it is, who it is for, link. Never more than 1 sales post a week |
| Countdown | Peak months (Aug to Nov) | Short, supportive: "还有 N 天, 今天只做一件事" |
| Poll or question | 2 a month | Use Telegram polls to ask what they struggle with; feeds the next video |

Rules: no spam, no more than about 1 post a day, never a sales post on a week with no value post. Write like a friendly senior, never preachy.

## Part 4b. Organising the channel (added 22 Nov 2026, channel is now open)
- **No bot for now.** The channel is one-way, so there is nothing for a bot to manage. Add one later only if the discussion group gets spam or you need an automatic welcome message.
- **Two pinned posts** (pin the Start here post first, welcome second): 1) "Start here" with links and hashtag index; 2) the welcome post (Part 3).
- **Hashtag index:** put the same tags on every post so students can tap a tag and see all posts of that kind: #新视频 #笔记 #考试倒数 #读书方法 #选科 #每周一封信 #问答. Tags are Gary's index; no bot needed.
  - **#选科 = choosing a stream or course only** (Science or Arts, which university course). **#读书方法 = how to study** (study methods, subject tips). Gary corrected this on 22 Nov 2026: he first read #选科 as "how to study".
- **Questions go through the website, not the chat.** Point to "Say hi" → "Ask Gary a question" (needs a free account). Why: the question lands in the website inbox, flows into the Notion Audience Questions list, and Gary's reply stays attached to it. Telegram comments are for quick reactions only.
- **Feedback:** reactions on every post (free signal), one Telegram poll every two weeks ("下个月你最想看哪一个？"), and the website's "Tell me your result" page for results.
- **Comments:** link the existing group as the Discussion. Set a short pinned rule in that group: no spam, no selling, no sharing paid notes, questions go to the website.
- **Weekly rhythm:** one letter, one video link, at most one product post (Part 4).
